# Wuzhen PBR Textures

The following texture maps are downloaded from Poly Haven and used by the Wuzhen 3D exhibition.
Poly Haven assets are licensed under CC0.

- `damaged_plaster`: weathered white wall
- `clay_roof_tiles_02`: aged roof tile
- `dark_wooden_planks`: timber structure
- `cobblestone_floor_01`: stone paving
- `denim_fabric_03`: indigo-dyed fabric
